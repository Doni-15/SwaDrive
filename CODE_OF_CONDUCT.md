# Code of Conduct

SwaDrive contributors are expected to communicate respectfully and keep
discussion focused on the work.

Expected behavior:

- critique ideas, not people;
- provide reproducible technical context;
- accept technical disagreement without personal attacks;
- avoid harassment and discriminatory language;
- respect privacy and do not publish credentials, private data, or another
  person's identifying information.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, unsafe, or off-topic contributions and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Security reports must
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public discussion threads.
